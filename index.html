Company overview

An ICT partner built on delivery, not just installs.

Innovative Solutions & Electronics is a 100% black-owned ICT company committed to enabling Skills Development Providers (SDPs) to deliver quality, technology-driven learning.

We understand that modern training requires modern infrastructure. Our core service is equipping SDPs with reliable ICT Infrastructure to ensure seamless learning, assessment, and QCTO accreditation compliance.

In addition to our ICT solutions, we have a dedicated division specialising in the supply and installation of solar products. This ensures that your training centre remains operational and cost-efficient, with uninterrupted power for learning — aligning with sustainable and cost-effective energy solutions.

Our team consists of skilled ICT professionals passionate about empowering education through technology. We pride ourselves on delivering compliant, scalable, and innovative solutions that not only meet QCTO and SETA infrastructure requirements but exceed your operational expectations.

At Innovative Solutions & Electronics, we are more than just an ICT supplier — we are your strategic partner in building a fully equipped, future-ready, and sustainable SDP.

Mission & vision

What we're working toward.

MISSION

Innovative Solutions & Electronics is a 100% black-owned ICT company committed to enabling Skills Development Providers (SDPs) to deliver quality, technology-driven learning.

Our mission is to provide reliable, compliant, and scalable ICT infrastructure that ensures seamless learning, assessment, and QCTO accreditation compliance through:

  • Equipping: Supplying high-performance Learner & Facilitator Devices, Projectors, and Interactive Whiteboards.
  • Connecting: Delivering full Networking & Connectivity solutions including LAN/WAN, Wi-Fi, Server Configuration, and Internet.
  • Enabling: Setting up complete Computer Labs and providing E-Learning infrastructure for LMS, Online Assessments, and Virtual Classrooms.
  • Sustaining: Ensuring operational continuity through our dedicated solar products division, providing cost-effective and uninterrupted power for learning.

Driven by skilled ICT professionals, we deliver solutions that not only meet QCTO and SETA requirements but exceed operational expectations.

VISION

  • To be the leading strategic ICT partner for Skills Development Providers (SDPs) in South Africa, enabling a future where every training centre is fully equipped, future-ready, compliant, and sustainably powered to deliver quality, technology-driven learning.
